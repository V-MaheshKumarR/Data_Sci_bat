MLOps Concepts
🟡

Staging

Newly trained model. Artifact stored but not serving traffic. Must pass eval threshold before promotion.

🟢

Production

The single active model serving all /predict requests. Promotion is atomic — old production auto-archives.

⚫

Archived

Superseded production model. Kept for rollback. Auto-pruned after 10 archived versions.
